## Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026. Univid collects only what it needs to turn links into videos, keeps it briefly, and never sells it. This policy explains the details.

### 1. What we collect

- **Discord identifiers:** user, server, channel, and message IDs for messages Univid processes or responds to.
- **Message content:** Univid reads messages in channels it can access only to detect supported video links. Messages without a supported link are discarded immediately and never stored.
- **Links and videos:** the links you submit and the videos downloaded from them.
- **Logs:** timestamps, commands used, the site a link points to, whether processing succeeded, and error details.
- **Server settings** that administrators configure.
- **Premium status:** whether a user or server has an active subscription, as reported by Discord. We never receive payment details.

We do not collect your email address, IP address, or Discord password.

### 2. How we use it

We use this data to process your requests, prevent abuse and enforce rate limits, fix errors, enforce our Terms, respond to copyright complaints, and apply premium features. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

### 3. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Original downloaded files | Deleted after compression, within 30 days |
| Compressed videos (cache) | 30 days, so the same link posted again isn't reprocessed |
| Logs | 30 days |
| Server settings | Until Univid is removed from the server, then deleted within 30 days |
| Block records for users or servers that violated the Terms | As long as needed to enforce the block |

### 4. Who we share it with

- **Discord:** videos Univid posts are visible to everyone in that channel and stored by Discord under Discord's Privacy Policy.
- **Service providers:** our hosting provider, Railway, stores and processes data on our behalf.
- **Legal requirements:** we may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users or others.
- **Transfers:** if Univid is transferred to a new operator, its data may transfer with it under this policy.

### 5. Source sites

When Univid downloads a video, the source site sees a request from our servers, not from you. That site's own privacy policy governs its content.

### 6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data, such as restricted server access and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure.

### 7. Children

Univid is not directed at children under 13, or under Discord's minimum age where you live. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

### 8. Your choices and rights

- **Access or deletion:** email chance@chancecallahan.com from a contact you can verify, with your Discord user ID, to request a copy or deletion of data tied to it. We respond within 30 days.
- **Posted videos:** deleting the Discord message removes it from the server. Discord controls any copies it retains.
- **Removing Univid:** server administrators can remove Univid at any time; its settings are then deleted as described above.
- **Regional rights:** depending on where you live, such as the EU, UK, or California, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your data. Where the GDPR applies, we process data based on our legitimate interest in operating Univid and on providing the service you request.

### 9. Where data is processed

Univid's servers are located in the United States. By using Univid, you understand your data may be processed there.

### 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date and announce material changes in the Univid support server (https://discord.gg/JDTDdBGdM).

### 11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: chance@chancecallahan.com or the Univid support server at https://discord.gg/JDTDdBGdM.
